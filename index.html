How this is measured

  • Every hour, all new offers per EAN for the Netherlands are read from bol.com: seller, regular price and who holds the buy box.
  • Select prices (the price bol.com Select members pay) are only shown on the public price overview page. That page is captured every morning at 06:35, so a Select price here is from that capture, not from the last hour.
  • RRP is the price for a box of six in the Lehmann web shop. For a glass that is not in the web shop, it is the distributor price list RRP times six.
  • A change counts from two cents. A seller who stops offering a listing and comes back counts as a change. "Below RRP since" runs as long as the seller stayed below RRP while offering the listing.
  • Times are Amsterdam time. A grey band in the chart means no measurement for more than three hours.
